Strategy at the center

The 6 outer tactics only compound when they're connected by one core strategy — your ideal client, your core message, and your story.

Without that center, each channel runs on its own. Money goes out, leads don't come in. With it, every channel reinforces the next.

We'll diagnose where your business is strong, weak, and missing — and rebuild the system around your strategy.

The customer journey

The Marketing Hourglass — aligned with how people actually buy.

Every prospect moves through 7 stages — from "who are you?" to "I'd happily refer you." Most businesses optimize for two of them and ignore the rest. We design across all seven.

KNOW
Message, ads, referrals, networking, earned media
LIKE
Website, blog, social media, podcast, articles
TRUST
SEO, webinars, marketing materials, white papers
TRY
Workshops, evaluations, demos, starter offers
BUY
Service team, new customer kit, onboarding
REPEAT
Project reviews, cross-selling, customer events
REFER
Champion events, partner intros, peer roundtables

Our process

How we build your marketing strategy.

Six structured steps. Roughly 30–45 days. You walk away with a complete, documented marketing plan and a 90-day execution calendar.

1

Kickoff Meeting

Align on scope, fees, timeline, and the people we'll need to talk to inside and outside your company.

2

Internal & Client Interviews

We talk to your team and your best customers. Why they bought. Why they stayed. What almost stopped them.

3

Online Presence Audit

Website, SEO, social, reviews, content, and competitors — scored honestly against what a buyer would see.

4

Brand Message Creation

Your core message, talking points, and the way you describe what you do — built from what customers actually said.

5

Marketing Plan Development

A documented strategy with personas, customer journey, content pillars, channels, and a 90-day calendar.

6

Coordinate Implementation

Walkthrough with your team and a clear handoff — whether you run it internally or we run it for you.

What we do in the first 30 days

The engagement — clear, contained, no surprises.

No black box. No 60-page proposal nobody reads. Three concrete deliverables in the first month that prove the strategy before we touch tactics.

01

Agree to scope and fees
